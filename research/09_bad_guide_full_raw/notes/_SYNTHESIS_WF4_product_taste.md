## 2. DELTAS (genuinely new beyond ESTABLISHED + WF3, deduped, no-overkill)

Each: finding · tag · prd_section · recommendation · source (with verbatim).

### NEW-1. §12 / positioning — Name the anti-pattern "desiccated simplicity," not "complexity"
The established positioning bans "-er-than-LangChain" and frames the foil as the bloated/heavyweight framework. Ive supplies a *sharper and different* negative noun: you can strip all the clutter and still ship something dead. *"Simplicity is trying to succinctly express the essence of something… Not removing clutter."* / the Valley's failure mode is *"a desiccated, soulless product."* For VERSO this names a foil the current vocabulary misses — not the heavyweight framework, but the **minimal-API framework that achieved emptiness instead of essence**: 3-line setup, nothing to hold onto when the agent breaks at 2am. "Desiccated simplicity" is a knife that cuts *toward* VERSO (we kept the essence: the seam, the eval surface, the inspect affordance) and away from both the bloated foil AND the merely-bare foil.
- tag: positioning · §12 (+ §2) · recommendation: **adopt-now** (add "desiccated simplicity" as the named anti-pattern; it is distinct from the bloat critique and from "knives-not-avocado-slicers")
- source: Jony Ive, Stripe Sessions 2025 — *"a desiccated, soulless product"*; *"expressing essence, not removing clutter."*

### NEW-2. §7 / founder_os — The "press-release / narrative-violation" test as a FEATURE-granularity gate
Established/WF3 has the *macro-coherence* test (Jobs/WWDC: "how does this fit a cohesive larger vision?") as a vision-level filter, and "non-goals are evidence-updatable." Fadell adds a strictly-more-operational artifact at *feature* granularity: before adding any primitive, write the one sentence it would occupy in the VERSO press release; if it forces a new premise — if a reader of the current press release would say "wait, I thought VERSO was about X" — it fails and does not ship. *"The story doesn't exist to sell your product. It's there to help you define it… write the press release before you build."* This is the buildable companion to the macro-coherence test (vision-gate) — it gives the firewall a concrete pass/fail object instead of a judgment call.
- tag: founder_os · §7 (feature-gating) + research/04_bad_guide_full · recommendation: **adopt-now** (use the press-release sentence as a feature-admission gate; complements, does not duplicate, the vision-level macro-coherence test)
- source: Tony Fadell × Tim Ferriss #590 — *"write the press release… it's there to help you define it"*; *"if a feature can't be in the press release without rewriting the premise, it doesn't belong in V1."*

### NEW-3. §1 / bet_moat — Reframe the eval-corpus flywheel as a "behavioral-context repository"
Established has the eval-corpus-aggregation flywheel ("we accumulate eval data → moat"). Field gives a structurally stronger framing: as cogeneration improves, the thing that wins is the *context repository the agents query for what-good-looks-like*. *"The better cogeneration gets, the more design matters… Figma [is] the context repository of aesthetics."* VERSO's analogue: **the behavioral-context repository** — agents run against VERSO's accumulated, opinionated definition of correct behavior (invariants + eval corpus + HITL seams), exactly as a Figma Make call queries live design tokens via MCP. This upgrades the moat sentence from "we have more eval data over time" (a data-accumulation claim) to "we are the surface agents run their correctness against" (a structural-role claim) — a sharper, more defensible §1 line.
- tag: bet_moat · §1 · recommendation: **adopt-now** (sharpen the flywheel into the "behavioral-context repository" role; pair with the existing mental-model-capture moat layer)
- source: Dylan Field, "Taste is your Moat" (Latent Space) — *"the better cogeneration gets, the more design matters and the human pushing on design matters too."*

### NEW-4. §12 / gtm_distribution — Position on DENOMINATOR compression (Value Equation), not feature numerators
Established frames the wedge as the struggling-moment upgrade and the demo as epistemic. Hormozi adds a concrete positioning *algebra* not previously named: `Value = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort)`. VERSO competes in the **denominator** — *time-to-reliability* (from "I want reliable prod agents" to "they actually are") and *effort-to-reliability* (the lift of rolling your own harness). The one-flag durable promotion and inspect() literally shrink both. The GTM corollary: buyers don't yet *perceive* the effort of building harness-from-scratch as high (they haven't felt it) — so the copy must make the cost of the alternative visible (this connects to, but is sharper than, the established struggling-moment frame: name the denominator explicitly). Hormozi's "perceived likelihood > actual success rate" also re-grounds why the measured-lift numbers and the 5 production-lead testimonials are *perceived-likelihood infrastructure*, not just references.
- tag: gtm_distribution · §12 · recommendation: **sharpen** (frame value as denominator-compression — time + effort to reliability — not feature count; this is positioning substance, the pricing mechanics are GTM apparatus → §3)
- source: Alex Hormozi, $100M Offers — *"Value = Dream Outcome × Likelihood / (Time Delay × Effort)"*; *"perceived likelihood matters more than actual success rate."*

### NEW-5. §12 / §1 — Adopt the DHM sentence as VERSO's canonical one-line strategy
Biddle's *"delight customers in hard-to-copy, margin-enhancing ways"* is a tighter strategy sentence than anything currently in the PRD's framing, and it forces three answers VERSO already has: delight (inspect(), one-flag durable, embedded evals) / hard-to-copy (process power + switching-costs-at-seam + behavioral-context repository) / margin-enhancing (time recovered + incident blast-radius reduced for the 2nd/3rd agent, not the 1st). The 8-type hard-to-copy taxonomy also gives a clean internal audit: VERSO's moat = **Process Power + Switching Costs + nascent Economies of Scale** (eval corpus grows per production agent). This is a sharpen of the existing moat articulation, not a new moat — but the *sentence* is worth adopting verbatim as the strategy header.
- tag: bet_moat / positioning · §1 + §12 · recommendation: **sharpen** (write VERSO's strategy in DHM form as the header sentence; map the moat to Biddle's 8 types as an internal clarity doc)
- source: Gibson Biddle — *"delight customers in hard-to-copy, margin-enhancing ways"*; counter-positioning = *"a business model incumbents can't copy"* (No-Late-Fees) → reinforces "we don't force the graph" as structural counter-position.

### NEW-6. §2 / §7 — "Two-product problem" (UX vs governance) names what the seam SOLVES for enterprise
Chennapragada's enterprise framing — every enterprise AI product must satisfy two opposing audiences simultaneously (UX delight vs governance/audit/rollback) — gives a *precise sentence* for what the `--durable` flag does that the established "tactical↔durable seam" language doesn't make explicit: the seam is the **governance affordance that doesn't compromise the UX surface** (one flag, not a rewrite, for the auditable/resumable/HITL upgrade). This is a §2/§7 sharpening (what the non-goal of "no forced graph" *enables*: governance-without-rewrite) and a concrete enterprise positioning line. Lightweight — one sentence, do not build an enterprise section around it.
- tag: positioning · §2 (+ §7, §12 enterprise copy) · recommendation: **sharpen** (name the seam as "governance without a rewrite — the answer to the two-product problem")
- source: Aparna Chennapragada (Microsoft CPO) — the enterprise "two-product problem"; *"prompt sets are the new PRDs"* (re-confirms evals-as-executable-spec, already established).
